# Project Specification: Logistika Express

Synthetic training document RAFT-P016. The client, people and figures are fictional.

## 1. Context and objectives

Logistika Express wants a parcel tracking dashboard. The objective is to replace manual handling of parcels and reduce errors.

## 2. Parties

The client is Logistika Express. The client's project contact address is pm.raftp016@example.com, and the phone number for urgent issues is +216 66 503 427. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 In scope

- Users can notify customers by email when a parcel is out for delivery.
- Users can assign parcels to delivery rounds.
- Users can let drivers update parcel status from a phone browser.
- Users can import parcels from a CSV file.
- Users can show a daily delivery performance report.

### 3.2 Out of scope

- Migration of historical data.
- Multi-currency pricing.
- Native mobile applications.

### 3.3 Change requests

Any feature that is not listed in section 3.1 is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 3 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## 4. Non-negotiable requirements

- NN-1: Monthly availability is at least 99.5%.
- NN-2: Personal data is stored only on servers located in Tunisia.
- NN-3: No personal data appears in application logs.
- NN-4: The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 30 days.

## 5. Milestones and payments

- M1, due at the end of week 2: clickable prototype and data model. Payment: 10% of the price.

The client has 10 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 1 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.
