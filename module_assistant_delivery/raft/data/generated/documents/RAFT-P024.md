## 1. Context and objectives

Médiathèque Ennour wants a book loan management app. The objective is to replace manual handling of loans and reduce errors.

## 2. Parties

The client is Médiathèque Ennour. The client's project contact address is pm.raftp024@example.com, and the phone number for urgent issues is +216 47 550 909. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 In scope

- Users can renew a loan online once.
- Users can show monthly loan statistics.
- Users can reserve a book that is on loan.
- Users can search the catalogue by title, author or subject.
- Users can send overdue reminders by email.

### 3.2 Out of scope

- Migration of historical data.
- Native mobile applications.
- Online card payment; customers pay on site.

### 3.3 Change requests

Any feature that is not listed in section 3.1 is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 3 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## 4. Non-negotiable requirements

- NN-2: Monthly availability is at least 99.9%.
- NN-3: Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.2 level AA.
- NN-4: The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 30 days.
