# Project Specification: Logistika Express

Synthetic training document RAFT-P030. The client, people and figures are fictional.

## 1. Context and objectives

Logistika Express wants a parcel tracking dashboard. The objective is to replace manual handling of parcels and reduce errors.

## 2. Parties

The client is Logistika Express. The client's project contact address is pm.raftp030@example.com, and the phone number for urgent issues is +216 84 206 102. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 In scope

- Users can let drivers update parcel status from a phone browser.
- Users can import parcels from a CSV file.
- Users can track a parcel with its tracking number.
- Users can notify customers by email when a parcel is out for delivery.
- Users can assign parcels to delivery rounds.

### 3.2 Out of scope

- Migration of historical data.
- A loyalty programme.
- Home delivery.

### 3.3 Change requests

Any feature that is not listed in section 3.1 is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 3 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## 4. Non-negotiable requirements

- NN-1: At most 15 parcels per delivery round.
- NN-2: Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.2 level AA.
- NN-3: Every administrator account uses two-factor authentication.
- NN-4: Personal data is stored only on servers located in Tunisia or the European Union.

## 5. Milestones and payments

- M1, due at the end of week 10: clickable prototype and data model. Payment: 20% of the price.
- M2, due at the end of week 12: main user flow working on staging. Payment: 30% of the price.
- M3, due at the end of week 13: back-office, notifications and test report. Payment: 30% of the price.
- M4, due at the end of week 17: production deployment and handover documentation. Payment: 20% of the price.

## 6. Acceptance

The client has 10 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 3 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.

## 7. Contract terms

- Fixed price: 20,000 TND excluding VAT, paid per milestone according to section 5. Invoices are payable within 30 days.
- Intellectual property on the delivered application transfers to the client after the final payment.
- Warranty: the freelancer fixes defects reported within 30 days after final acceptance at no extra cost.
- Termination: either party may terminate with 30 days of written notice.
- Confidentiality: both parties keep business information confidential for 2 years after the project ends.
- Late delivery: a penalty of 1% of the price per week of delay applies, capped at 10%.

## 8. Communication

A 30-minute progress call takes place every Wednesday. Decisions that change scope, price or deadlines are confirmed in writing.
