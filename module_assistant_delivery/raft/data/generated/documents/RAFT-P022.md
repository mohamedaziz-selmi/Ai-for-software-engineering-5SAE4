## 1. Context and objectives

Atelier Bois & Lin wants a click-and-collect furniture shop. The objective is to replace manual handling of orders and reduce errors.

## 2. Parties

The client is Atelier Bois & Lin. The client's project contact address is pm.raftp022@example.com, and the phone number for urgent issues is +216 66 991 777. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 In scope

- Users can manage stock levels in a back-office.
- Users can browse products by room and material.
- Users can send an email when an order is ready.
- Users can choose a collection slot at the warehouse.
- Users can add products to a cart and place an order.

### 3.2 Out of scope

- Native mobile applications.
- Home delivery.
- Online card payment; customers pay on site.

### 3.3 Change requests

Any feature that is not listed in section 3.1 is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 2 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## 4. Non-negotiable requirements

- NN-1: Personal data is stored only on servers located in the European Union.
- NN-2: The home page reaches Largest Contentful Paint in under 3 seconds on a simulated 4G connection.
- NN-3: The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 14 days.
- NN-4: No personal data appears in application logs.
