# Project Specification: Studio Atlas Fitness

Synthetic training document RAFT-P006. The client, people and figures are fictional.

## 1. Context and objectives

Studio Atlas Fitness wants a class booking web app. The objective is to replace manual handling of bookings and reduce errors.

## 2. Parties

The client is Studio Atlas Fitness. The client's project contact address is pm.raftp006@example.com, and the phone number for urgent issues is +216 46 308 214. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 In scope

- Users can browse the weekly class timetable.
- Users can let coaches view their attendee list.
- Users can join a waiting list when a class is full.
- Users can let staff create and edit classes.
- Users can receive a reminder email 24 hours before a class.

### 3.2 Out of scope

- Multi-currency pricing.
- Migration of historical data.
- Native mobile applications.

### 3.3 Change requests

Any feature that is not listed in section 3.1 is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 2 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## 4. Non-negotiable requirements

- NN-1: Personal data is stored only on servers located in Tunisia or the European Union.
- NN-2: Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.
- NN-3: Monthly availability is at least 99.5%.
- NN-4: The home page reaches Largest Contentful Paint in under 2.5 seconds on a simulated 4G connection.

## 5. Milestones and payments

- M1, due at the end of week 5: clickable prototype and data model. Payment: 10% of the price.
- M2, due at the end of week 6: main user flow working on staging. Payment: 40% of the price.
- M3, due at the end of week 7: back-office, notifications and test report. Payment: 30% of the price.

## 6. Acceptance

The client has 7 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 1 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.

## 7. Contract terms

- Warranty: the freelancer fixes defects reported within 60 days after final acceptance at no extra cost.
- Termination: either party may terminate with 30 days of written notice.
- Confidentiality: both parties keep business information confidential for 1 years after the project ends.
