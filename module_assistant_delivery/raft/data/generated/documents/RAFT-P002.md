## 1. Context and objectives

Rent&Go Sfax wants a car rental reservation portal. The objective is to replace manual handling of reservations and reduce errors.

## 2. Parties

The client is Rent&Go Sfax. The client's project contact address is pm.raftp002@example.com, and the phone number for urgent issues is +216 44 383 143. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 In scope

- Users can send a confirmation email with the reservation number.
- Users can search available cars by date and category.
- Users can upload a scan of the driving licence.
- Users can let agents confirm or decline reservations.
- Users can reserve a car and choose a pickup agency.

### 3.2 Out of scope

- Native mobile applications.
- A loyalty programme.
- Multi-currency pricing.

### 3.3 Change requests

Any feature that is not listed in section 3.1 is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 2 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## 4. Non-negotiable requirements

- NN-1: No personal data appears in application logs.
- NN-2: Passwords are hashed with Argon2id.
- NN-3: The home page reaches Largest Contentful Paint in under 2 seconds on a simulated 4G connection.
- NN-4: Personal data is stored only on servers located in Tunisia or the European Union.
