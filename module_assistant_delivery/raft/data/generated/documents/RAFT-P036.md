# Project Specification: Logistika Express

Synthetic training document RAFT-P036. The client, people and figures are fictional.

## 1. Context and objectives

Logistika Express wants a parcel tracking dashboard. The objective is to replace manual handling of parcels and reduce errors.

## 2. Parties

The client is Logistika Express. The client's project contact address is pm.raftp036@example.com, and the phone number for urgent issues is +216 87 620 820. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 In scope

- Users can notify customers by email when a parcel is out for delivery.
- Users can let drivers update parcel status from a phone browser.
- Users can show a daily delivery performance report.
- Users can assign parcels to delivery rounds.
- Users can track a parcel with its tracking number.

### 3.2 Out of scope

- Migration of historical data.
- A loyalty programme.
- Online card payment; customers pay on site.

### 3.3 Change requests

Any feature that is not listed in section 3.1 is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 5 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## 4. Non-negotiable requirements

- NN-1: No personal data appears in application logs.
- NN-2: At most 30 parcels per delivery round.
- NN-3: Monthly availability is at least 99.5%.
- NN-4: The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 7 days.

## 5. Milestones and payments

- M1, due at the end of week 3: clickable prototype and data model. Payment: 25% of the price.
- M2, due at the end of week 8: main user flow working on staging. Payment: 25% of the price.
- M3, due at the end of week 12: back-office, notifications and test report. Payment: 25% of the price.
- M4, due at the end of week 20: production deployment and handover documentation. Payment: 25% of the price.

## 6. Acceptance

The client has 10 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 2 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.

## 7. Contract terms

- Fixed price: 19,000 TND excluding VAT, paid per milestone according to section 5. Invoices are payable within 30 days.
- Intellectual property on the delivered application transfers to the client after the final payment.
- Warranty: the freelancer fixes defects reported within 60 days after final acceptance at no extra cost.
- Termination: either party may terminate with 15 days of written notice.
- Confidentiality: both parties keep business information confidential for 2 years after the project ends.

## 8. Communication

A 45-minute progress call takes place every Monday. Decisions that change scope, price or deadlines are confirmed in writing.
