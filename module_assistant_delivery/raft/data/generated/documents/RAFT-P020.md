# Project Specification: Studio Atlas Fitness

Synthetic training document RAFT-P020. The client, people and figures are fictional.

## 1. Context and objectives

Studio Atlas Fitness wants a class booking web app. The objective is to replace manual handling of bookings and reduce errors.

## 2. Parties

The client is Studio Atlas Fitness. The client's project contact address is pm.raftp020@example.com, and the phone number for urgent issues is +216 71 418 921. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 In scope

- Users can browse the weekly class timetable.
- Users can book and cancel a class.
- Users can join a waiting list when a class is full.
- Users can let staff create and edit classes.
- Users can receive a reminder email 24 hours before a class.

### 3.2 Out of scope

- Migration of historical data.
- Native mobile applications.
- Online card payment; customers pay on site.

### 3.3 Change requests

Any feature that is not listed in section 3.1 is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 3 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## 4. Non-negotiable requirements

- NN-1: The home page reaches Largest Contentful Paint in under 3 seconds on a simulated 4G connection.
- NN-2: Every administrator account uses two-factor authentication.
- NN-3: No personal data appears in application logs.
- NN-4: Personal data is stored only on servers located in Tunisia or the European Union.

## 5. Milestones and payments

The client has 10 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 2 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.

- Late delivery: a penalty of 2% of the price per week of delay applies, capped at 10%.
