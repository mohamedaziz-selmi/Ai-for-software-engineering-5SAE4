# Project Specification: Atelier Bois & Lin

Synthetic training document RAFT2-P050. The client, people and figures are fictional.

## 1. Purpose of the project

Atelier Bois & Lin wants a click-and-collect furniture shop. The goal is to stop handling orders by hand and reduce errors.

## 2. Parties

The client is Atelier Bois & Lin. Project questions go to pm.raft2p050@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 56 312 284. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## 3. Scope

### Included

- Choose a collection slot at the warehouse.
- Users can browse products by room and material.
- Manage stock levels in a back-office.
- Add products to a cart and place an order.
- Send an email when an order is ready.

### Not included

- Multi-currency pricing.
- A loyalty programme.
- Native mobile applications.

## 4. Changes to the scope

Features not described above are handled as change requests. The freelancer sends an estimate within 3 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## 5. Mandatory requirements

- NN-1: The home page reaches Largest Contentful Paint in under 2.5 seconds on a simulated 4G connection.
- NN-2: Personal data is stored only on servers located in Tunisia.
- NN-3: Monthly availability is at least 99.9%.
- NN-4: Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.2 level AA.
