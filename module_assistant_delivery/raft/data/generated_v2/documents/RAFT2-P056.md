# Project brief - Rent&Go Sfax

Synthetic training document RAFT2-P056. The client, people and figures are fictional.

### Project overview

Rent&Go Sfax wants a car rental reservation portal. The goal is to stop handling reservations by hand and reduce errors.

### Parties and contacts

The client is Rent&Go Sfax. Project questions go to pm.raft2p056@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 23 444 562. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

### Functional scope

Planned features: reserve a car and choose a pickup agency; let agents confirm or decline reservations; send a confirmation email with the reservation number; upload a scan of the driving licence; show a fleet availability calendar to staff.

Expressly excluded from this project: a loyalty programme; online card payment; migration of historical data.

### Change requests

Any feature that is not listed in the scope is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 3 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

### Mandatory requirements

- Passwords are hashed with scrypt.
- Monthly availability is at least 99.9%.
- The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 30 days.
- At most 20 reservations per pickup hour.

### Schedule and payments

- M1, due at the end of week 5: clickable prototype and data model. Payment: 10% of the price.
- M2, due at the end of week 12: main user flow working on staging. Payment: 40% of the price.
- M3, due at the end of week 14: back-office, notifications and test report. Payment: 30% of the price.
- M4, due at the end of week 20: production deployment and handover documentation. Payment: 20% of the price.

### Review and acceptance

The client has 3 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 3 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.

### Commercial terms

Fixed price: 24,500 TND excluding VAT, paid per milestone. Invoices are payable within 45 days.

Intellectual property on the delivered application transfers to the client after the final payment. Warranty: the freelancer fixes defects reported within 90 days after final acceptance at no extra cost. Termination: either party may terminate with 30 days of written notice.

Both parties keep business information confidential for 2 years after the project ends.

Late delivery: a penalty of 0.5% of the price per week of delay applies, capped at 10%.

### Project governance

A 45-minute progress call takes place every Monday. Decisions that change scope, price or deadlines are confirmed in writing.
