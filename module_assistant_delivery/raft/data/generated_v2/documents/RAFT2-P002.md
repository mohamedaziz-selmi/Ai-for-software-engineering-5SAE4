# Project Specification: Atelier Bois & Lin

## 1. Context and objectives

Atelier Bois & Lin wants a click-and-collect furniture shop. The goal is to stop handling orders by hand and reduce errors.

## 2. Parties

The client is Atelier Bois & Lin. Project questions go to pm.raft2p002@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 44 259 332. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## 3. Scope of work

### In scope

- Let staff mark orders as ready for collection.
- Send an email when an order is ready.
- Users can choose a collection slot at the warehouse.
- Browse products by room and material.
- Users can manage stock levels in a back-office.

### Out of scope

- Migration of historical data.
- A loyalty programme.
- Native mobile applications.

## 4. Additional requests

Any feature that is not listed in the scope is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 2 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## 5. Non-negotiable requirements

- NN-1: The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 7 days.
- NN-2: Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.
- NN-3: At most 20 orders per collection slot.
- NN-4: Monthly availability is at least 99%.

The client has 3 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 1 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.

Intellectual property on the delivered application transfers to the client after the final payment. Warranty: the freelancer fixes defects reported within 30 days after final acceptance at no extra cost. Termination: either party may terminate with 15 days of written notice.
