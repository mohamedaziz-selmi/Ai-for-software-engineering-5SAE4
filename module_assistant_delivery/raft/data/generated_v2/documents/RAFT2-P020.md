Today Logistika Express manages its parcels with spreadsheets and phone calls. This project delivers a parcel tracking dashboard.

## Article 2 - Who is involved

The client is Logistika Express. The client's project contact address is pm.raft2p020@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 46 194 449. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## Article 3 - Functional scope

The project covers the following features: notify customers by email when a parcel is out for delivery; track a parcel with its tracking number; let drivers update parcel status from a phone browser; show a daily delivery performance report; assign parcels to delivery rounds.

Expressly excluded from this project: migration of historical data; multi-currency pricing; native mobile applications.

## Article 4 - Additional requests

Features not described above are handled as change requests. The freelancer sends an estimate within 3 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

4. At most 10 parcels per delivery round.
