## Article 1 - Project overview

Today CoWork Lac manages its bookings with spreadsheets and phone calls. This project delivers a desk and meeting-room booking app.

## Article 2 - Parties and contacts

The client is CoWork Lac. The client's project contact address is pm.raft2p044@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 60 823 300. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## Article 3 - Functional scope

Planned features: book a desk or a meeting room; let managers set booking rules per member plan; block rooms for maintenance; receive a monthly usage summary by email; check in with a QR code at the desk.

Expressly excluded from this project: a loyalty programme; online card payment; home delivery.

## Article 4 - Change requests

Features not described above are handled as change requests. The freelancer sends an estimate within 5 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## Article 5 - Mandatory requirements

1. The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 14 days.
2. At most 20 bookings per one-hour slot.
3. Passwords are hashed with scrypt.
4. Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.2 level AA.
