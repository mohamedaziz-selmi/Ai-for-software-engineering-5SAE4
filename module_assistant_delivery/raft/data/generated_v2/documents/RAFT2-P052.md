# Project Specification: Studio Atlas Fitness

Synthetic training document RAFT2-P052. The client, people and figures are fictional.

## Article 1 - Project overview

Today Studio Atlas Fitness manages its bookings with spreadsheets and phone calls. This project delivers a class booking web app.

## Article 2 - Parties

The client is Studio Atlas Fitness. The client's project contact address is pm.raft2p052@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 49 969 556. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## Article 3 - Functional scope

The first release will: browse the weekly class timetable; let staff create and edit classes; join a waiting list when a class is full; book and cancel a class; receive a reminder email 24 hours before a class.

Expressly excluded from this project: migration of historical data; home delivery; a loyalty programme.

## Article 4 - Additional requests

Features not described above are handled as change requests. The freelancer sends an estimate within 2 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## Article 5 - Mandatory requirements

1. Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.
2. At most 25 bookings per class session.
3. Personal data is stored only on servers located in the European Union.
4. Monthly availability is at least 99.5%.

## Article 6 - Delivery plan

Milestone M1 is due at the end of week 5 and covers the clickable prototype and data model; it triggers payment of 20% of the price.
Milestone M2 is due at the end of week 8 and covers the main user flow working on staging; it triggers payment of 30% of the price.
Milestone M3 is due at the end of week 11 and covers the back-office, notifications and test report; it triggers payment of 30% of the price.
Milestone M4 is due at the end of week 20 and covers the production deployment and handover documentation; it triggers payment of 20% of the price.

## Article 7 - Validation of milestones

The client has 10 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 1 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.

## Article 8 - Contract terms

Fixed price: 29,500 TND excluding VAT, paid per milestone. Invoices are payable within 45 days.

Intellectual property on the delivered application transfers to the client after the final payment. Warranty: the freelancer fixes defects reported within 60 days after final acceptance at no extra cost. Termination: either party may terminate with 30 days of written notice.

Both parties keep business information confidential for 2 years after the project ends.

## Article 9 - Communication

A 30-minute progress call takes place every Wednesday. Decisions that change scope, price or deadlines are confirmed in writing.
