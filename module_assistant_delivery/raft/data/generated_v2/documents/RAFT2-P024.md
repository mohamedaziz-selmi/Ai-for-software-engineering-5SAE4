# Project brief - CoWork Lac

Synthetic training document RAFT2-P024. The client, people and figures are fictional.

### Background

CoWork Lac wants a desk and meeting-room booking app. The goal is to stop handling bookings by hand and reduce errors.

### Parties

The client is CoWork Lac. Project questions go to pm.raft2p024@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 69 763 415. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

### Functional scope

Planned features: receive a monthly usage summary by email; book a desk or a meeting room; check in with a QR code at the desk; let managers set booking rules per member plan; see a floor plan with free desks.

Expressly excluded from this project: native mobile applications; online card payment; a loyalty programme.

### Additional requests

Any feature that is not listed in the scope is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 3 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

### Essential requirements

- Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.
- Passwords are hashed with scrypt.
- The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 30 days.
- Personal data is stored only on servers located in the European Union.

### Validation of milestones

The client has 10 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 3 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.

### Contract terms

Fixed price: 17,000 TND excluding VAT, paid per milestone. Invoices are payable within 15 days.

Intellectual property on the delivered application transfers to the client after the final payment. Warranty: the freelancer fixes defects reported within 30 days after final acceptance at no extra cost. Termination: either party may terminate with 15 days of written notice.

Both parties keep business information confidential for 3 years after the project ends.

### Meetings
