# Project Specification: Dar Lughat Language School

Synthetic training document RAFT2-P032. The client, people and figures are fictional.

### Background

Today Dar Lughat Language School manages its enrolments with spreadsheets and phone calls. This project delivers a course enrolment platform.

### Who is involved

The client is Dar Lughat Language School. The client's project contact address is pm.raft2p032@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 46 710 888. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

### Functional scope

The first release will: download an enrolment certificate as PDF; let teachers record attendance; browse courses by language and level; let staff manage course groups and rooms; take an online placement test.

Expressly excluded from this project: multi-currency pricing; native mobile applications; online card payment.

### Additional requests

Any feature that is not listed in the scope is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 5 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

### Mandatory requirements

- Personal data is stored only on servers located in Tunisia.
- Monthly availability is at least 99%.
- Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.
- Passwords are hashed with Argon2id.

### Milestones and payments

- M1, due at the end of week 9: clickable prototype and data model. Payment: 20% of the price.
- M2, due at the end of week 10: main user flow working on staging. Payment: 30% of the price.
- M3, due at the end of week 11: back-office, notifications and test report. Payment: 30% of the price.
- M4, due at the end of week 19: production deployment and handover documentation. Payment: 20% of the price.

### Acceptance

The client has 10 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 1 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.

### Contract terms

Fixed price: 13,500 TND excluding VAT, paid per milestone. Invoices are payable within 30 days.

Intellectual property on the delivered application transfers to the client after the final payment. Warranty: the freelancer fixes defects reported within 90 days after final acceptance at no extra cost. Termination: either party may terminate with 15 days of written notice.

Both parties keep business information confidential for 1 years after the project ends.

Late delivery: a penalty of 1% of the price per week of delay applies, capped at 5%.

### Project governance

A 45-minute progress call takes place every Monday. Decisions that change scope, price or deadlines are confirmed in writing.
