# Service agreement: a car rental reservation portal for Rent&Go Sfax

Synthetic training document RAFT2-P034. The client, people and figures are fictional.

## 1. Project overview

Rent&Go Sfax wants a car rental reservation portal. The goal is to stop handling reservations by hand and reduce errors.

## 2. Who is involved

The client is Rent&Go Sfax. The client's project contact address is pm.raft2p034@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 75 121 781. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## 3. Functional scope

### Included

- Search available cars by date and category.
- Let agents confirm or decline reservations.
- Users can upload a scan of the driving licence.
- Show a fleet availability calendar to staff.
- Send a confirmation email with the reservation number.

### Out of scope

- Native mobile applications.
- Home delivery.
- Multi-currency pricing.

## 4. Change requests

Any feature that is not listed in the scope is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 2 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## 5. Non-negotiable requirements

- NN-1: Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.
- NN-2: At most 10 reservations per pickup hour.
- NN-3: Personal data is stored only on servers located in Tunisia.
- NN-4: Monthly availability is at least 99.5%.
