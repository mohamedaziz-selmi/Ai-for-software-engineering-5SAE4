### Functional scope

Planned features: manage opening hours and closed days; cancel a reservation from the confirmation link; choose a date, time and party size; let staff see tonight's reservations by table; export the daily reservations as CSV.

Expressly excluded from this project: multi-currency pricing; online card payment; a loyalty programme.

### Additional requests

Features not described above are handled as change requests. The freelancer sends an estimate within 5 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

- Personal data is stored only on servers located in the European Union.
- The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 7 days.
