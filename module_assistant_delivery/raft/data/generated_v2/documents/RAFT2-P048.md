### Background

Dar Lughat Language School wants a course enrolment platform. The goal is to stop handling enrolments by hand and reduce errors.

### Parties

### Scope of work

Planned features: download an enrolment certificate as PDF; enrol in a course group; browse courses by language and level; let staff manage course groups and rooms; let teachers record attendance.

The following are not part of the project: multi-currency pricing; a loyalty programme; home delivery.
