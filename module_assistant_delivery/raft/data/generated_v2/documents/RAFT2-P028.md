# Project brief - Café Jasmin

Synthetic training document RAFT2-P028. The client, people and figures are fictional.

## Article 1 - Background

Today Café Jasmin manages its table reservations with spreadsheets and phone calls. This project delivers a table reservation website.

## Article 2 - Who is involved

The client is Café Jasmin. The client's project contact address is pm.raft2p028@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 62 925 815. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## Article 3 - Scope of work

Planned features: export the daily reservations as CSV; let staff see tonight's reservations by table; receive an SMS confirmation; cancel a reservation from the confirmation link; choose a date, time and party size.

The following are not part of the project: a loyalty programme; migration of historical data; online card payment.

## Article 4 - Additional requests

Any feature that is not listed in the scope is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 3 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## Article 5 - Essential requirements

1. The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 14 days.
2. The home page reaches Largest Contentful Paint in under 3 seconds on a simulated 4G connection.
3. Passwords are hashed with Argon2id.
4. At most 20 table reservations per 30-minute seating.

## Article 6 - Milestones and payments

Milestone M1 is due at the end of week 2 and covers the clickable prototype and data model; it triggers payment of 10% of the price.
Milestone M2 is due at the end of week 4 and covers the main user flow working on staging; it triggers payment of 40% of the price.
Milestone M3 is due at the end of week 14 and covers the back-office, notifications and test report; it triggers payment of 30% of the price.
Milestone M4 is due at the end of week 17 and covers the production deployment and handover documentation; it triggers payment of 20% of the price.

## Article 7 - Acceptance

The client has 7 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 3 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.

## Article 8 - Contract terms

Fixed price: 9,000 TND excluding VAT, paid per milestone. Invoices are payable within 15 days.

Intellectual property on the delivered application transfers to the client after the final payment. Warranty: the freelancer fixes defects reported within 60 days after final acceptance at no extra cost. Termination: either party may terminate with 15 days of written notice.

Both parties keep business information confidential for 3 years after the project ends.

Late delivery: a penalty of 1% of the price per week of delay applies, capped at 10%.

## Article 9 - Meetings

A 60-minute progress call takes place every Monday. Decisions that change scope, price or deadlines are confirmed in writing.
