# Project brief - Café Jasmin

Synthetic training document RAFT2-P012. The client, people and figures are fictional.

## Article 1 - Background

Café Jasmin wants a table reservation website. The goal is to stop handling table reservations by hand and reduce errors.

## Article 2 - Who is involved

The client is Café Jasmin. Project questions go to pm.raft2p012@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 96 314 573. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## Article 3 - Functional scope

The first release will: cancel a reservation from the confirmation link; let staff see tonight's reservations by table; export the daily reservations as CSV; choose a date, time and party size; manage opening hours and closed days.

The following are not part of the project: multi-currency pricing; home delivery; migration of historical data.

## Article 4 - Changes to the scope

Features not described above are handled as change requests. The freelancer sends an estimate within 5 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## Article 5 - Essential requirements

1. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt.
2. Monthly availability is at least 99.9%.
3. Personal data is stored only on servers located in Tunisia.
4. The home page reaches Largest Contentful Paint in under 2 seconds on a simulated 4G connection.
