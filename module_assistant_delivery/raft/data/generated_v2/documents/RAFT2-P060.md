# Project Specification: Studio Atlas Fitness

Synthetic training document RAFT2-P060. The client, people and figures are fictional.

## Article 1 - Context and objectives

Studio Atlas Fitness wants a class booking web app. The goal is to stop handling bookings by hand and reduce errors.

## Article 2 - Who is involved

The client is Studio Atlas Fitness. Project questions go to pm.raft2p060@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 29 977 895. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## Article 3 - Scope of work

The first release will: let coaches view their attendee list; receive a reminder email 24 hours before a class; browse the weekly class timetable; join a waiting list when a class is full; let staff create and edit classes.

The following are not part of the project: online card payment; native mobile applications; multi-currency pricing.

## Article 4 - Additional requests

Features not described above are handled as change requests. The freelancer sends an estimate within 3 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## Article 5 - Mandatory requirements

1. Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.
2. The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 30 days.
3. Monthly availability is at least 99.5%.
4. The home page reaches Largest Contentful Paint in under 3 seconds on a simulated 4G connection.

The client has 5 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 1 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.

Intellectual property on the delivered application transfers to the client after the final payment. Warranty: the freelancer fixes defects reported within 30 days after final acceptance at no extra cost. Termination: either party may terminate with 15 days of written notice.

Both parties keep business information confidential for 3 years after the project ends.

A 30-minute progress call takes place every Wednesday. Decisions that change scope, price or deadlines are confirmed in writing.
