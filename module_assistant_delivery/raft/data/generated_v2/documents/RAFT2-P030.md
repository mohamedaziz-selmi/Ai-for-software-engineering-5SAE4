## 4. Change requests

Any feature that is not listed in the scope is a change request. The freelancer sends an estimate within 2 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## 5. Non-negotiable requirements

- NN-1: Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.
- NN-2: Monthly availability is at least 99%.
- NN-3: The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 7 days.
- NN-4: Personal data is stored only on servers located in the European Union.
