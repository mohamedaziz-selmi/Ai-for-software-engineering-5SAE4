# Project Specification: Café Jasmin

Synthetic training document RAFT2-P064. The client, people and figures are fictional.

### Context and objectives

Café Jasmin wants a table reservation website. The goal is to stop handling table reservations by hand and reduce errors.

### Who is involved

The client is Café Jasmin. Project questions go to pm.raft2p064@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 31 333 957. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

### Functional scope

Planned features: manage opening hours and closed days; receive an SMS confirmation; choose a date, time and party size; cancel a reservation from the confirmation link; let staff see tonight's reservations by table.

The following are not part of the project: migration of historical data; online card payment; multi-currency pricing.

### Change requests

Features not described above are handled as change requests. The freelancer sends an estimate within 5 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

### Essential requirements

- Personal data is stored only on servers located in the European Union.
- Monthly availability is at least 99%.
- The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 30 days.
- Passwords are hashed with scrypt.

### Milestones and payments

- M1, due at the end of week 2: clickable prototype and data model. Payment: 20% of the price.
- M2, due at the end of week 12: main user flow working on staging. Payment: 30% of the price.
- M3, due at the end of week 13: back-office, notifications and test report. Payment: 30% of the price.
- M4, due at the end of week 17: production deployment and handover documentation. Payment: 20% of the price.

### Validation of milestones

The client has 7 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 1 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.

### Legal and financial terms

Fixed price: 37,000 TND excluding VAT, paid per milestone. Invoices are payable within 30 days.

Intellectual property on the delivered application transfers to the client after the final payment. Warranty: the freelancer fixes defects reported within 60 days after final acceptance at no extra cost. Termination: either party may terminate with 15 days of written notice.

Both parties keep business information confidential for 3 years after the project ends.

### Project governance

A 45-minute progress call takes place every Monday. Decisions that change scope, price or deadlines are confirmed in writing.
