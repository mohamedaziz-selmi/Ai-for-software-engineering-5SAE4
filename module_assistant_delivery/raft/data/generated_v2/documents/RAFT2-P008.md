### Project overview

Dar Lughat Language School wants a course enrolment platform. The goal is to stop handling enrolments by hand and reduce errors.

### Parties

### Scope

The project covers the following features: download an enrolment certificate as PDF; let teachers record attendance; let staff manage course groups and rooms; browse courses by language and level; take an online placement test.

Expressly excluded from this project: online card payment; migration of historical data; native mobile applications.

### Changes to the scope

Features not described above are handled as change requests. The freelancer sends an estimate within 5 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

### Non-negotiable requirements

- Personal data is stored only on servers located in the European Union.
- The database is backed up every day and backups are kept for 14 days.
- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt.
- Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.
