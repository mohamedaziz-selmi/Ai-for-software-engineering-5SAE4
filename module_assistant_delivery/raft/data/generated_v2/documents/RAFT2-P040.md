# Service agreement: a table reservation website for Café Jasmin

Synthetic training document RAFT2-P040. The client, people and figures are fictional.

### Project overview

Café Jasmin wants a table reservation website. The goal is to stop handling table reservations by hand and reduce errors.

### Parties and contacts

The client is Café Jasmin. The client's project contact address is pm.raft2p040@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 99 105 551. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

### Scope

The project covers the following features: export the daily reservations as CSV; manage opening hours and closed days; choose a date, time and party size; cancel a reservation from the confirmation link; receive an SMS confirmation.

Expressly excluded from this project: multi-currency pricing; native mobile applications; online card payment.

### Additional requests

Features not described above are handled as change requests. The freelancer sends an estimate within 5 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

### Mandatory requirements

- At most 10 table reservations per 30-minute seating.
- Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.
- Monthly availability is at least 99.9%.
- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt.

### Milestones and payments

- M1, due at the end of week 2: clickable prototype and data model. Payment: 30% of the price.
- M2, due at the end of week 3: main user flow working on staging. Payment: 30% of the price.
- M3, due at the end of week 5: back-office, notifications and test report. Payment: 20% of the price.
- M4, due at the end of week 15: production deployment and handover documentation. Payment: 20% of the price.

### Acceptance

The client has 7 business days to review each milestone. A milestone is accepted when no blocking defect remains. Each milestone includes up to 3 rounds of revisions; further rounds are change requests.

### Legal and financial terms

Fixed price: 37,500 TND excluding VAT, paid per milestone. Invoices are payable within 15 days.

Intellectual property on the delivered application transfers to the client after the final payment. Warranty: the freelancer fixes defects reported within 30 days after final acceptance at no extra cost. Termination: either party may terminate with 15 days of written notice.

Both parties keep business information confidential for 1 years after the project ends.

### Meetings

A 60-minute progress call takes place every Wednesday. Decisions that change scope, price or deadlines are confirmed in writing.
