## 1. Project overview

Today Atelier Bois & Lin manages its orders with spreadsheets and phone calls. This project delivers a click-and-collect furniture shop.

## 2. Parties

The client is Atelier Bois & Lin. Project questions go to pm.raft2p058@example.com; urgent issues can be reported by phone on +216 32 218 749. The freelancer designs, builds, tests and deploys the application.

## 3. Scope of work

### In scope

- Send an email when an order is ready.
- Users can browse products by room and material.
- Choose a collection slot at the warehouse.
- Users can add products to a cart and place an order.
- Let staff mark orders as ready for collection.

### Not included

- Migration of historical data.
- A loyalty programme.
- Native mobile applications.

## 4. Change requests

Features not described above are handled as change requests. The freelancer sends an estimate within 3 business days, and work starts only after written approval by the client.

## 5. Non-negotiable requirements

- NN-1: Customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.
- NN-2: At most 25 orders per collection slot.
- NN-3: Personal data is stored only on servers located in the European Union.
- NN-4: Passwords are hashed with scrypt.
