Accessibility audit report, milestone M3
Project PRJ-DEMO-001, Atelier Lumière online ordering. Synthetic demonstration document.

Standard: WCAG 2.1 level AA.
Scope: the customer-facing pages (catalogue, cart, pickup slot picker, account and password reset, order history).
Method: automated scan of every page with axe-core 4.10, one keyboard-only pass and one screen-reader pass (NVDA).

Results
- Catalogue: pass. Every product image has a text alternative.
- Cart: pass.
- Pickup slot picker: one issue found and fixed during the audit. Full slots were shown by colour only;
  a "Complet" label now appears next to them.
- Account and password reset: pass.
- Order history: pass.

Open issues: none.
Conclusion: at the end of M3 the customer-facing pages meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.
